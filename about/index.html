About the game

What waits in Prophecies

A slow-burning game of medieval statecraft. You play one character across a living map of thirty-six settlements, where five great powers keep a running ledger of every favour, slight, and debt you leave behind.

The world

The realm turns on a prophecy no one can quite agree on. The nights are growing longer, the sun dimming stage by stage, and the powers that rule the land are more concerned with their own count of debts than with the dark gathering at the edges of the map.

You begin as one person — a name, a birthplace, a handful of obligations — and make your way through a web of settlements, courts, and rivalries. Build a reputation, raise a household, take a throne if you can hold it, or let the long night come and see who is left standing when it does.

The five powers

Every power keeps count of what you owe. Fall behind with one, and the others take notice.

  • The CrownLaw, taxation, and the right to name heirs.
  • The FaithKeepers of the prophecy — and of who gets absolved.
  • The GuildsCoin, contracts, and the roads between settlements.
  • The BannersSworn houses and the swords they can call.
  • The CourtFavour, glory, and the knife that follows.

The cultures

Andric

Chainmail and obligation. A people bound by oath, ledger, and the weight of what is owed.

Sessau

Court, glory, and the knife after. Brilliant, ambitious, and never quite safe to turn your back on.

Nightcourt

Not yet known to the map. Some say they are only a rumour. The rumour is spreading.

How it plays

One character. Thirty-six settlements. Turn by turn, you decide where to go, whom to serve, and whom to cross — and the map remembers. There is no single path to the throne, and more than one way to lose it.
