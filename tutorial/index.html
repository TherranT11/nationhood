The General Elections

Front Sessau is a right-wing, nationalist party. They are currently in a coalition with Union Conservatrice for a weak majority.

Last month, Prime Minister Clément de Sylvie resigned after evidence of his allegations of siphoning millions of dollars in defense contracts to foreign military conglomerates in exchange for kickbacks.

With Clément de Sylvie’s resignation, you have won an unsteady party leadership election. Now the opposition is threatening a vote of no confidence, but your coalition partner UC is willing to wait until the upcoming elections.
