    Trade Policy

    The import-side ladder — how open the nation is to foreign trade. Each rung sets the import multiplier (the buyer's sticker × the world rate), the tariff (% withheld from the seller and banked as the buyer's customs revenue), and optional per-tick effects. Nations adopt a rung through the legislature; this defines what each rung does. The starred rung is the default for nations that haven't legislated a change.

    Party Inactivity

    Wall-clock since each party's last action (any action, vote, conviction adoption, or proposal). Players are warned in-game at 6 days; the party goes inactive at 7 (it sits out elections) and is auto-deleted at 21 — the 8-hour tick removes it and its politicians and frees its seats. Any action revives it. You can also delete a party manually here at any time.

    Crises

    A crisis fires when all its trigger conditions are met, then climbs a meter through five stages — each stage adds growth per tick, and management actions push it back down. Reaching the terminal stage applies its effect.
