    Policies

    A policy populates every nation. An On/Off policy toggles between two states, each carrying its own stat effects. A Spectrum policy is an ordered ladder of levels — moving between them takes implementation time, and each level’s effects sum across the levels crossed. Proposing a change costs Action Points, scaled by the popularity the vote swings (set below). Effects are stored now and applied once the ministry-stat backend lands.

    Stat Connectors

    Couple any ministry stat to any other, for every nation. A connector reads: Every time Source increases by Y, Target rises / falls by A — measured from a reference value. The target simply gains a live term A × ⌊(source − reference) ÷ Y⌋, recomputed wherever the stat shows, so it reverses when the source falls and never loops (a rule reads its source’s value before any connector applies). Growth, Prosperity and Rule of Law move on the shown figure only for now.

    Trade Policy

    The import-side ladder — how open the nation is to foreign trade. Each rung sets the import multiplier (the buyer's sticker × the world rate), the tariff (% withheld from the seller and banked as the buyer's customs revenue), and optional per-tick effects. Nations adopt a rung through the legislature; this defines what each rung does. The starred rung is the default for nations that haven't legislated a change.

    Loading…

    Party Inactivity

    Wall-clock since each party's last action (any action, vote, or proposal). Players are warned in-game at 6 days; the party goes inactive at 7 (it sits out elections) and is auto-deleted at 21 — the 6-hour tick removes it and its politicians and frees its seats. Any action revives it. You can also delete a party manually here at any time.
