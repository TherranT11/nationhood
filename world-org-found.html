Convene a New International Body

A new international body comes into being when its founding charter is signed by enough states to make it legitimate. As the convening state, you draft the charter, invite the founders, and host the inaugural summit. Done well, you create a lasting framework where your country sits at the center. Done badly, you stage a public humiliation no one will let you forget.
Only the Foreign Affairs & Trade Minister or the Head of Government can convene an organization.
