🎚️ 2. Option

A multi-option policy is built from one or more options. Each option is a complete configuration — its own sector effects, costs, and stat effects — that nations can switch between. Phase 2.4 lays in a single-option scaffold; multi-option support arrives in Phase 2.9.

🗳️ Sector Effects (up to 5)

Per-sector popularity shift on resolution. Pass: sponsor + YES voters get the change, NO voters get the inverse, abstain unaffected. Fail: sponsor takes the inverse alone.
Magnitude is 0.1 to 2.0 in 0.1 steps.

🌍 Starting Status

Which nations have this option active at game start? Active = ongoing cost runs, stat effects do NOT fire (stats are pre-set).
