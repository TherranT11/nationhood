Govern a nation. Shape a continent.

Nationhood is a multiplayer political simulation where every law, budget, and diplomatic deal is decided by real players. Found a party, win elections, form coalitions, and steer your nation through economic crises, trade wars, and the politics of survival.

Political Parties Playable Now

Create a political party, recruit a leader, and fight for seats in parliament. Draft legislation, negotiate coalitions, set tax policy, manage diplomacy, and try to hold power long enough to actually change something.

Elections & Campaigns
Run rallies, craft messaging, target voter blocs, and compete in multi-party elections with real seat math.
Legislation
Draft bills, whip votes, navigate committee review, and fight floor debates to pass or block policy.
Coalitions & Governance
No majority? Negotiate coalition deals, divide ministries, and hold a fragile government together.
Diplomacy & Trade
Appoint ambassadors, propose trade agreements, set tariffs, and manage relations with other player-run nations.
Corporations Coming Soon

Build a corporate empire inside a player-driven economy. Lobby governments for favorable regulation, compete for market share across sectors, and navigate the intersection of profit and politics.

Corporate gameplay is in development. Sectors, trade flows, and tariff systems are already live — corporations will plug directly into the existing economic simulation.

Military Branches Coming Soon
