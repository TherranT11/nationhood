This is the Government screen. It generally looks different, but because there is currently no government, it shows the Government Formation UI instead. As you are the largest party, you have an opportunity to form a coalition first.

A coalition is when two or more parties join together to form a single government. You can see that Government Confidence has already taken a hit because of 3 crises affecting the nation. We will address those shortly. For now, look at the different options to form a coalition.

Forming larger coalitions make a more sturdier coalition, but parties that are not aligned to your own Archetype bring Confidence down. Each party has it's own demands in order to join your coalition.
