II.Acquire New Assets

Build From Scratch ◊ Or Purchase Existing

Build a Regional HQ

A Regional HQ lets your corporation operate in another nation. Cost is $300M base × the host nation's standard-of-living multiplier (clamped between ×0.5 and ×2.0). The HQ becomes a standard property asset — sell it later from your assets list at condition-adjusted resale (purchase price × current condition %).

Build a Light Assembly Plant

Assembles airframes for your certified designs. Cost is $175M base × the host nation's cost-of-living multiplier (clamped between ×0.5 and ×2.0). Requires an existing Regional HQ presence in the target nation. The plant becomes a standard property asset — same condition-adjusted resale as any other building.

Build an Engine Assembly Plant

Builds the engines that power your aircraft. Cost is $225M base × the host nation's cost-of-living multiplier (clamped between ×0.5 and ×2.0). Requires an existing Regional HQ presence in the target nation. The plant becomes a standard property asset — same condition-adjusted resale as any other building.

Build an Aircraft Assembly Facility

Final-stage integration: combines airframes and engines into completed aircraft ready for delivery. Cost is $225M base × the host nation's cost-of-living multiplier (clamped between ×0.5 and ×2.0). Requires an existing Regional HQ presence in the target nation. The facility becomes a standard property asset — same condition-adjusted resale as any other building.

Build a Heavy Manufacturing Plant

The structural backbone of every aircraft — builds 1 airframe at a time (Narrowbody or Widebody). Cost is $375M base × the host nation's cost-of-living multiplier (clamped between ×0.5 and ×2.0). Requires an existing Regional HQ presence in the target nation. The plant becomes a standard property asset — same condition-adjusted resale as any other building.

Buy Existing Property

Purchase a property already on the market in your home nation. Faster than construction (instant occupancy on next tick), and inherits the seller's condition — which feeds into the resale value if you flip it.
