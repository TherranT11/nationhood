# Debug Guide: Budget Bills & Government Shutdown

---

## How The System Works (Quick Reference)

### Budget Bill Lifecycle
1. **Player-initiated only** — budget bills are never auto-generated by tick processing. A faction must click "Submit Budget Bill" on the Bills tab.
2. Bill enters `committee` status, then moves to `floor` for voting.
3. `BUDGET_BILL_VOTING_TICKS: null` — budget bills **never expire**. They persist in committee/floor until explicitly passed or voted down.
4. When passed: `resolveBudgetBill()` updates `last_budget_tick` on the nation, adjusts debt/reserves.
5. When failed: bill status set to `failed`, nothing else happens (no budget applied, no `last_budget_tick` update).

### Government Shutdown Lifecycle
1. **Trigger:** `isGovernmentShutdown()` checks if any budget bill (status = `committee` or `floor`) has been open for **2+ ticks**.
2. **Continues:** Every tick the bill remains open (not passed/failed), shutdown penalties apply.
3. **Ends:** When there are **zero** open budget bills (all passed or failed). `resolveGovernmentShutdown()` deletes the `active_crises` row.
4. **No timeout** — shutdown has NO auto-expiration. It lasts forever until the bill resolves.

### Key Gotcha: What If a 2004 Budget Isn't Passed by 2006?

**If the bill is still sitting in committee/floor:** The shutdown continues indefinitely. Since `BUDGET_BILL_VOTING_TICKS: null`, the bill never expires. It will sit there forever, keeping the shutdown active, until someone passes or votes it down.

**If the bill was voted down (failed):** The shutdown ENDS immediately because `isGovernmentShutdown()` finds zero open budget bills. However, `processNoBudgetPenalty()` kicks in instead, applying escalating efficiency/stability/credit penalties because `last_budget_tick` is stale.

**If a NEW budget bill is submitted while the old one is open:** Both exist. `isGovernmentShutdown()` only looks at the OLDEST open budget bill (`ORDER BY proposed_tick ASC LIMIT 1`). The shutdown timer is based on that oldest bill's `proposed_tick`.

### Penalty Summary

| System | Effect | Rate |
|--------|--------|------|
| Shutdown: Events | `adjustGovernmentApprovalEvent` | -5/tick |
| Shutdown: Ministers | `SHUTDOWN_MINISTER_PENALTY` | -6/tick per minister |
| Shutdown: Composite | Flat penalty on `calculateGovernmentApprovalTick` | -25 during shutdown |
| Shutdown: Stability | Direct stat damage | -1/tick |
| Shutdown: Institutions | All institutions forced to 0% funding | Collapsed decay rates |
| No-Budget: Efficiency | Scaling penalty after 12 ticks w/o budget | up to -2.0/tick |
| No-Budget: Stability | Scaling penalty after 12 ticks w/o budget | up to -1.5/tick |
| No-Budget: Credit | Scaling penalty after 12 ticks w/o budget | up to -1.0/tick |

## Debug Queries

### 1. Check All Open Budget Bills For a Nation

```sql
-- Find all budget bills currently in committee or floor (keeping shutdown alive)
SELECT
    b.id,
    b.bill_name,
    b.status,
    b.proposed_tick,
    b.voting_ends_tick,
    b.passed_tick,
    b.early_resolution_status,
    s.current_tick AS current_tick,
    (s.current_tick - b.proposed_tick) AS ticks_open,
    CASE
        WHEN (s.current_tick - b.proposed_tick) >= 2 THEN 'SHUTDOWN ACTIVE'
        ELSE 'Grace period (< 2 ticks)'
    END AS shutdown_status
FROM bills b
CROSS JOIN (SELECT current_tick FROM shard WHERE name = 'Alpha Shard') s
WHERE b.nation_id = '<NATION_ID>'
  AND b.bill_type = 'budget'
  AND b.status IN ('committee', 'floor')
ORDER BY b.proposed_tick ASC;
```

### 2. Check Budget Bill History (All Statuses)

```sql
-- Full history of all budget bills for the nation
SELECT
    b.id,
    b.bill_name,
    b.status,
    b.proposed_tick,
    b.passed_tick,
    b.voting_ends_tick,
    f.faction_name AS proposed_by
FROM bills b
LEFT JOIN factions f ON f.id = b.proposed_by
WHERE b.nation_id = '<NATION_ID>'
  AND b.bill_type = 'budget'
ORDER BY b.proposed_tick DESC;
```

### 3. Check Nation's Last Budget Tick & Overdue Status

```sql
-- How long since a budget was passed?
SELECT
    n.name,
    n.last_budget_tick,
    n.last_budget_bill_id,
    s.current_tick,
    (s.current_tick - COALESCE(n.last_budget_tick, 0)) AS ticks_since_last_budget,
    CASE
        WHEN (s.current_tick - COALESCE(n.last_budget_tick, 0)) > 12
        THEN 'OVERDUE (no-budget penalties active)'
        ELSE 'OK'
    END AS budget_status,
    n.budget_reserves,
    n.debt
FROM nations n
CROSS JOIN (SELECT current_tick FROM shard WHERE name = 'Alpha Shard') s
WHERE n.id = '<NATION_ID>';
```

### 4. Check Active Government Shutdown Crisis

```sql
-- Is the shutdown crisis record active?
SELECT
    ac.id,
    ac.crisis_id,
    ac.started_at_tick,
    s.current_tick,
    (s.current_tick - ac.started_at_tick) AS ticks_active,
    ac.effects_applied_log
FROM active_crises ac
CROSS JOIN (SELECT current_tick FROM shard WHERE name = 'Alpha Shard') s
WHERE ac.nation_id = '<NATION_ID>'
  AND ac.crisis_id = '00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000001';
```

### 5. Check Government Approval Components

```sql
-- Breakdown of composite government approval
SELECT
    n.name,
    n.gov_approval,
    n.gov_approval_institutional,
    n.gov_approval_outcomes,
    n.gov_approval_events,
    ROUND(COALESCE(n.gov_approval_institutional, 0) * 0.45
        + COALESCE(n.gov_approval_outcomes, 0) * 0.35
        + (50 + COALESCE(n.gov_approval_events, 0)) * 0.20) AS computed_composite
FROM nations n
WHERE n.id = '<NATION_ID>';
```

### 6. Check All Minister Approvals

```sql
-- Minister approval breakdown (institutional component input)
SELECT
    m.ministry_key,
    m.minister_first_name || ' ' || m.minister_last_name AS minister_name,
    m.minister_approval,
    m.embattled_since_tick,
    CASE
        WHEN m.minister_approval < 20 THEN 'CRISIS (< 20)'
        WHEN m.minister_approval < 30 THEN 'EMBATTLED (< 30)'
        WHEN m.minister_approval < 50 THEN 'Below average'
        ELSE 'OK'
    END AS status,
    CASE
        WHEN m.embattled_since_tick IS NOT NULL
        THEN (SELECT current_tick FROM shard WHERE name = 'Alpha Shard') - m.embattled_since_tick
        ELSE NULL
    END AS ticks_embattled
FROM ministries m
WHERE m.nation_id = '<NATION_ID>'
  AND m.is_active = true
ORDER BY m.minister_approval ASC;
```

### 7. Check Gov Approval Event Log (Recent History)

```sql

```

### 9. Bill Support Breakdown For Open Budget Bills

```sql
-- Who's voting on the budget bill and how?
SELECT
    bs.faction_id,
    f.faction_name,
    bs.stance,
    bs.seat_count
FROM bill_support bs
JOIN factions f ON f.id = bs.faction_id
JOIN bills b ON b.id = bs.bill_id
WHERE b.nation_id = '<NATION_ID>'
  AND b.bill_type = 'budget'
  AND b.status IN ('committee', 'floor')
ORDER BY bs.seat_count DESC;
```

### 10. Full Shutdown Timeline (Event Log)

```sql
-- All shutdown-related events
SELECT
    event_name,
    description_used,
    fired_at_tick,
    effects_applied,
    created_at
FROM event_log
WHERE nation_id = '<NATION_ID>'
  AND (event_name LIKE '%Shutdown%' OR event_name LIKE '%Budget%' OR event_name LIKE '%shutdown%')
ORDER BY fired_at_tick DESC
LIMIT 20;
```

## Common Scenarios & What To Look For

### Scenario: Shutdown running for 20+ ticks, approval still above 30%
**Check:** Query #5 (approval components) and #6 (minister approvals). If minister approvals are still high, the institutional component (45% weight) is propping up the composite. The -6/tick shutdown penalty + -25 composite penalty should now fix this.

### Scenario: Shutdown won't end even though bill was voted down
**Check:** Query #1 (open budget bills). There might be a SECOND open budget bill still in committee/floor. `isGovernmentShutdown()` only needs ONE open bill to keep shutdown active.

### Scenario: Budget bill stuck in committee forever
**Expected behavior:** Budget bills have `voting_ends_tick: null`, so they never auto-expire. They must be voted on (moved to floor and resolved). If no one votes, the bill sits in committee indefinitely, and after 2 ticks the shutdown triggers.

### Scenario: No shutdown but nation stats still tanking
**Check:** Query #3 (last budget tick). If `ticks_since_last_budget > 12`, the `processNoBudgetPenalty()` is applying escalating efficiency/stability/credit drops even without a shutdown.

### Scenario: New budget bill submitted but shutdown persists
**Expected behavior:** Submitting a new budget bill does NOT end the shutdown. The new bill must be PASSED. While it's in committee/floor, the shutdown continues (based on the oldest open bill's age).
