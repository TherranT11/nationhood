Variables — substituted when the event is drawn: {Nation} host country (e.g. Samgola) · {City} the embassy's location (e.g. Samgola Dawn) · {Name1} a person from the host nation's name list · {Corp} a corporation operating in the country. They work in the title, description, and decision descriptions.
