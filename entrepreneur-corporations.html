Banking

Shipping
Charter routes and move cargo. Cyclical, asset-heavy revenue; scales with Influence.
Real Estate
Acquire finished buildings from Construction corps and collect rent. Steady tenant revenue; scales with Reputation.
Airline
Buy aircraft and fly passenger routes between cities. Demand scales with each nation's standard of living. Capital-intensive — founds empty; buy your first aircraft from corp treasury. Expand beyond your HQ nation by building a Regional HQ.
