Welcome to Nationhood

Nationhood is a persistent online game that is largely populated with other players. We are excited to have you, but we want things to be civil.

These are the rules.

Be respectful.

Treat each other with respect and keep a friendly tone.

Keep it clean.

Keep chats appropriate for all ages. No offensive language or NSFW material.

Write in English.

Use English in all general channels and in-game. Language-specific channels are available.

No multiple accounts.

One account per person. Don't ruin it for everyone else.

No trolling.

Trolling will result in a warning, then an immediate ban. Good faith participation is required.

Take the game seriously.

Nationhood is a political simulation, not a meme generator. Naming your nation "Fartistan," electing President Deez Nuts, or submitting a Joint Statement that's a copypasta will earn a warning, then a ban.

Being within the rules but acting in bad faith will be called out, then action will be taken. Sometimes the rules can't account for everything, but breaking the spirit of the game isn't fun for anyone.

Do not use real corporations' names or logos in-game.
